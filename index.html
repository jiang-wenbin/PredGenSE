PredGenSE: Time-Unconditional Generative Speech Enhancement With Predictive Guidance Online Supplement

Authors

Wenbin Jiang, Zhen Wang, Yang Zhang, Wen Zhang, and Xiaofeng Huang

Abstract

Most existing DNN-based speech enhancement (SE) methods are predictive and may struggle to generalize to unseen acoustic conditions, whereas recently emerging diffusion- and flow-matching-based generative SE methods can introduce perceptual artifacts and timbre distortions because their training objectives are not always well aligned with speech quality. To address these limitations, we propose PredGenSE, a unified end-to-end framework that integrates generative--predictive learning with time-unconditional autonomous rectified flow within a single neural ODE formulation. Specifically, by leveraging a linear interpolation path, the per-sample target velocity becomes time-invariant, motivating PredGenSE to eliminate explicit time-step conditioning and instead learn a stationary mapping from the current state and noisy observation to the target residual. Furthermore, we incorporate a differentiable ODE solver into the training process, enabling waveform-level reconstruction losses to be back-propagated through the entire generative trajectory, thereby jointly optimizing both the generative dynamics and the final speech reconstruction. Extensive experiments on two standard speech enhancement benchmarks (VoiceBank+DEMAND and WSJ0+CHiME3) and a universal SE benchmark demonstrate that PredGenSE achieves competitive performance against recent representative predictive, generative, and hybrid methods with only one or five function evaluations.

Datasets

  • The VoiceBank+DEMAND dataset is used for demo.
  • The WSJ0+CHiME3 benchmark (High-SNR subset) is used for demo.
  • The universal SE benchmark uses the DNS Challenge 2021 synthetic no-reverb test set.
  • Audio samples of the test sets we processed are available at the repository (VCTK+DEMAND, WSJ0+CHiME3, and DNS2021 test sets).
